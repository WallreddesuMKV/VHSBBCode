WORKFLOW

QUICK START
  1. 1
    Pick Source: Select Steam, GOG, Epic, or itch.io from the top tab.
  2. 2
    Fetch Metadata: Enter App ID or store URL and click Fetch (or press Enter). Title, banner, quote, dev/publisher, tags, and languages populate automatically.
  3. 3
    Check Included Content: DLCs auto-fetch in batches with no item limit. You can also type or paste extra items manually (one per line).
  4. 4
    Repack & Links: Fill in Source/Crack, sizes, RAM required, and add your download mirrors (+ Add Link).
  5. 5
    Preview & Copy: Click Preview to view the rendered forum thread, then click Copy to copy clean BBCode to clipboard.
